Why vendored (not a pip/Flatpak dependency):
  * Single pure-Python file, zero compiled code, no third-party imports beyond
    the stdlib. Vendoring is lighter and lower-risk than a manifest source entry
    with its own build step — the same reasoning applied to flatlib.
  * Deterministic table-driven data; nothing to compile against the KDE SDK.

Range limitation (IMPORTANT):
  Chinese-calendar years 1900–2099 only. Solar dates outside roughly
  1900-01-31 .. 2100-ish raise ValueError. engines/lunar_calendar.py wraps every
  call and returns a graceful "unavailable" result outside this window rather
  than letting the exception surface. All living users are in range; historical
  (pre-1900) birth dates are not supported.

Consumed by:
  chs/engines/lunar_calendar.py  — the only module that imports this. Nothing
  else touches lunardate directly, so if the vendored lib is ever swapped, the
  engine is the single edit point.
